Clinical visits · lab A1C

Lab results are saved on this device only. A1C reflects roughly the last 3 months, so the 90-day GMI is the fairest comparison. A gap of ±0.5% between them is common. GMI = 3.31 + 0.02392 × average mg/dL (the current clinical standard). eA1C = (average + 46.7) ÷ 28.7, the older ADAG formula many loop apps such as Trio show — it reads lower than GMI at most averages, and apps also differ in the days they average over.

Hour by hour

Zero-COB stretches

Zero-COB hours have no carbs on board for the whole hour and no carbs logged in it, so glucose drift mostly reflects basal. A loop hides basal problems by changing temp basals, so read glucose drift together with how much insulin the loop delivered vs your schedule (basal + SMB). Rising, or the loop adding a lot, suggests scheduled basal may be low then. Falling, or the loop cutting a lot, suggests it may be high. Check the hour or two before a pattern, since basal acts with a delay. A meal bolus within 3 h is marked ⚠. These are patterns to discuss with your care team, not dose instructions.
